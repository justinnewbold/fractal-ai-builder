Privacy

Fractal Remote · last updated 18 September 2026

Used on your own wifi, this app sends nothing anywhere. Your phone talks to your computer, your computer talks to your unit, and nothing leaves the room — no account, no sign-in, nothing stored on anybody's server. That is how most people use it and it is worth saying first.

Everything below is about the parts that do use the internet, and each one is something you choose to turn on.

What is collected, and only if you ask for it

An account — only if you want to reach your rig from away

Playing at home over your own wifi needs no account. If you want your phone to reach your computer from somewhere else, the two ends have to find each other, and that needs a sign-in.

WhatWhy
Your email addressTo sign you in, and to reset a password
Your passwordStored as a hash. It is never readable, by us or anyone.

The commands between your phone and your computer pass through a relay while you are connected this way — a preset change, a scene, a knob. They are passed along, not written down.

Your setlists — only if you are signed in

So the setlist you built on the couch is on your phone at the gig. It holds the names and order of the songs you saved and which presets you starred. Nothing else about your presets, and no audio of any kind.

Bug reports and suggestions — only when you press Send

There are two kinds and they carry different things, deliberately.

Something is brokenI want something
What you wroteyesyes
Your email, if you typed oneoptionaloptional
App version, your unit, your OS, screen sizeyesyes
The debug logyes, and you can turn it offnever

The log is a record of what the app did — buttons pressed, requests made, errors. It is gathered the moment you press Send and at no other time, you can read the whole thing first, and you can send the report without it. A suggestion never carries one, and the database itself refuses to store one against a suggestion.

The log holds no preset contents, no audio, no account details, and nothing else you have typed into the app.

What is never collected

  • No analytics and no tracking of any kind. There is no analytics service in this app, no advertising identifier, and no third-party tracking code. Nobody is counting your screens or your sessions.
  • No location. Never asked for, never read.
  • No contacts, photos, or microphone. The camera is used only if you point it at a pairing code on your own screen, and nothing is saved or sent.
  • No audio. This app sends control messages to your unit. It does not record, stream or process sound.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with anyone for advertising or any other purpose.

Where it is kept, and who can see it

Accounts, setlists and reports are stored with Supabase, on servers in the United States. Every row is behind rules that tie it to the account that made it. Bug reports can be written by anybody and read back by nobody through the app — only by the person who builds it.

The desktop apps check for their own updates from GitHub, and the phone app checks for updates from Expo. Both are ordinary download requests.

How long it is kept

  • Your account and setlists — until you ask for them to be deleted.
  • Bug reports — until the problem is fixed and long enough afterwards to be sure, then deleted.

Deleting your data

Email justinnewbold@gmail.com and say what you want removed — your account, your setlists, a report you sent, or all of it. No form and no account needed to ask. It will be done and you will get a reply confirming it.

You can also delete your setlists from inside the app at any time without asking anyone.

Children

This app is for people with guitar hardware and is not directed at children under 13. No account is knowingly created for one. If you believe a child has signed up, email the address above and it will be removed.

Changes

If what this app collects ever changes, this page changes with it and the date at the top moves. Anything that would collect materially more than the above would be announced in the app before it happened, not buried here.
