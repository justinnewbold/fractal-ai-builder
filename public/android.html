Looking for the latest build…

One moment.

Tap the button on the phone itself. Android asks once whether this browser may install apps — say yes. It installs over an earlier one without removing anything, and your setlists and pairing stay where they are.

Most changes reach the app on their own and need none of this. A new build is only needed when something about the app itself changed — a new phone, or a fresh start.

Built for 64-bit Android, which is every phone made this decade. If an older handset says App not installed, that is why — tell me and I will add the older one back.
